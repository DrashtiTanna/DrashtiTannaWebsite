# Homepage illustration

The original drawing from Drashti's CV is preserved in `shared/assets/cv-landscape.jpg` and used as the terrain's static fallback.

The panoramic ink illustration at `shared/assets/cv-landscape-enhanced.webp` is an AI-refined derivative, not the unmodified original. It retains the CV's landscape, travel, architecture, and art motifs, with clearer charcoal linework and a deep-red world map. The generation also reinterpreted details, including travel balloons.

The illustration was edited with the built-in Image Gen tool, then encoded as a high-quality WebP at its original 2172 by 724 dimensions. The 102KB website asset preserves the composition during encoding. CSS crops blank vertical padding; the full panorama is horizontally explorable on small screens.

The edit brief was to preserve the panoramic hand-drawn identity while strengthening faint lines, retain charcoal and CV-red accents, avoid typography or photorealism, and keep both ends of the landscape inside the frame. A follow-up edit restored an opaque light-paper background matching `#faf9f6`.

The animated terrain is separate, code-native conceptual artwork. It does not depict a model of either featured project.
